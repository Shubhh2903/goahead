### Changed

- The app is now GoAhead, for internal use at Multi Code Genius. It signs in to the GoAhead account
  service and connects through the GoAhead servers, not the OpenBot ones.
- **Install each new version by hand from the GitHub release.** GoAhead does not update itself, and
  it sends no analytics.
- **GoAhead keeps its data in its own folder.** Data of an OpenBot app on the same computer is not
  moved or changed. Sign in and connect Slack again in GoAhead.
